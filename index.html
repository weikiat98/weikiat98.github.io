ABOUT ME

Who I Am

A problem-solver at heart and a detail-oriented individual, I experiment building GenAI Proof-of-Concepts (PoCs) through rapid prototyping to explore concepts and solve use cases. My GenAI journey started from joining IMDA GenAI Innovation Team to support the promotion and adoption of AI tools within IMDA. I train staff, promote digital transformation in IMDA through VIBE training, build solutions using IMDA's in-house GenAI MAGIC Platform, understanding user requirements and simplifying complex AI concepts to non-technical users I also gather user feedback to improve the tools usage and support the innovation of IMDA GenAI Magic Platform from UI/UX to AI Safety Testing with AWS.

I pursued a Bachelor's of Science Degree in Economics and Politics from the University of London, achieving Second Upper Class (Honours). During my undergraduate days, I developed a strong curiosity in uncovering the stories hidden within numbers and this analytical foundation proved essential in transitioning from theoretical Economics and Politics to the world of data analytics, and into the rapidly evolving field of GenAI. Subsequently, I pursued a Master's of Business in Analytics and Visualisation at the Singapore University of Social Science (SUSS), graduating with a CGPA of 4.46, gaining valuable exposure and skills in the fields of data science.

Beyond work, I enjoy gaming, gyming, photography and enjoy travelling away from the bustle of city life.
